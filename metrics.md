1. Identification & Classification

Basic identifiers and categorical features.

Ticker – Stock symbol on the exchange.
Company Name – Official company name.
Sector – GICS sector classification.
Industry – Sub-industry classification.

2. Price Data (Daily OHLCV)

The fundamental market data.

Open Price – Price at market open.
High Price – Highest price during the day.
Low Price – Lowest price during the day.
Close Price – Final price at market close.
Adjusted Close – Price adjusted for dividends/splits.
Volume – Number of shares traded.
Dollar Volume – Total value traded (price × volume).
VWAP – Volume-weighted average price for the day.

3. Size & Market Structure

Shares Outstanding – Total shares issued.
Float Shares – Shares available for public trading.
Market Capitalization – Price × shares outstanding.
Free Float Market Cap – Market cap excluding locked shares.
Enterprise Value (EV) – Market cap + debt − cash.
Market Cap Rank – Relative size ranking among stocks.
Cap Category – Mega / Large / Mid / Small / Micro.

4. Liquidity Metrics

Average Volume (30d) – Average shares traded over 30 days.
Average Volume (90d) – Average shares traded over 90 days.
Turnover Ratio – Volume / shares outstanding.
Bid-Ask Spread – Difference between best bid and ask.
Liquidity Score – Composite liquidity measure.

5. Return & Performance Metrics

Daily Return – Percentage change from previous close.
Weekly Return – 5-day price change.
Monthly Return – 30-day price change.
Quarterly Return – 3-month price change.
Yearly Return – 12-month price change.
Cumulative Return – Total return over the dataset period.

6. Momentum Factors

1-Month Momentum – Return over past 1 month.
3-Month Momentum – Return over past 3 months.
6-Month Momentum – Return over past 6 months.
12-Month Momentum – Return over past year (excluding recent month).
Price vs 50-Day MA – Current price relative to 50-day moving average.
Price vs 200-Day MA – Current price relative to 200-day moving average.

7. Volatility & Risk Metrics

Daily Volatility – Standard deviation of daily returns.
30-Day Volatility – Rolling 30-day return volatility.
90-Day Volatility – Rolling 90-day volatility.
Average True Range (ATR) – Average daily trading range.
Beta vs Market – Sensitivity to market index returns.
Downside Volatility – Volatility during negative returns only.
Maximum Drawdown – Largest peak-to-trough decline.

8. Dividend Metrics

Dividend Per Share (DPS) – Cash dividend paid per share.
Dividend Yield – Annual dividends divided by share price.
Dividend Payout Ratio – Dividends / net income.
Dividend Growth Rate – Growth rate of dividend payments.
Dividend Frequency – Annual / semi-annual / quarterly.
Ex-Dividend Date – Date when dividend eligibility ends.
Dividend Payment Date – Date dividend is paid.
Dividend History Length – Years of dividend payments.

9. Earnings & Profitability

Earnings Per Share (EPS) – Net income per share.
Forward EPS – Expected future earnings per share.
Net Profit Margin – Net income divided by revenue.
Operating Margin – Operating income divided by revenue.
Gross Margin – Gross profit divided by revenue.
Return on Equity (ROE) – Net income divided by equity.
Return on Assets (ROA) – Net income divided by total assets.
Return on Invested Capital (ROIC) – Profit relative to invested capital.

10. Valuation Metrics

Price-to-Earnings (PE) – Price divided by EPS.
Forward PE – Price divided by expected EPS.
Price-to-Book (PB) – Price divided by book value.
Price-to-Sales (PS) – Market cap divided by revenue.
EV / EBITDA – Enterprise value divided by EBITDA.
Earnings Yield – Earnings divided by price.
Book-to-Market Ratio – Book value relative to market value.

11. Growth Metrics

Revenue Growth (YoY) – Year-over-year revenue change.
EPS Growth (YoY) – Year-over-year earnings growth.
Free Cash Flow Growth – Growth in free cash flow.
Asset Growth – Increase in company assets.
Equity Growth – Growth in shareholder equity.

12. Balance Sheet Metrics

Total Assets – Total company assets.
Total Liabilities – Total debts and obligations.
Debt-to-Equity Ratio – Total debt divided by equity.
Current Ratio – Current assets divided by current liabilities.
Cash Ratio – Cash divided by current liabilities.
Net Debt – Total debt minus cash.

13. Cash Flow Metrics

Operating Cash Flow – Cash generated from operations.
Free Cash Flow – Operating cash flow minus capex.
FCF Yield – Free cash flow divided by market cap.
Capital Expenditure (CapEx) – Investment in assets.
Cash Flow Margin – Cash flow relative to revenue.

14. Market Sentiment (Optional but useful)

Short Interest – Percentage of shares sold short.
Short Interest Ratio – Days required to cover short positions.
Analyst Rating – Consensus analyst recommendation.
Price Target – Average analyst target price.
